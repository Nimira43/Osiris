About Us

Immersive Worlds Awakened

At Osiris, we believe in the power of immersive storytelling and evocative atmospheres. Founded in 2017 in Hereford by Matt Osbourne, Jack Irvine, and Steve Isaac, our journey began with Shadowdancer, a title that set the stage for redefining how players experience psychological horror and suspense.

From enigmatic puzzles to walking adventures like Labyrinth and Chloe’s Prophecy, our games weave narratives that resonate deeply. With every project, Osiris invites players into unforgettable realms where exploration is key, and every moment unravels a mystery worth discovering.
