About Us

Immersive Worlds Awakened

Osiris was founded in 2017 in Hereford by Matt Osbourne, Jack Irvine and Steve Isaac. Shadowdancer was the first of our atmospheric video games that still lingers in the mind. Follow up games like Labyrinth and Chloe's Prophesy redefine psychological horror and suspense.

Specialising in immersive experiences, we weave puzzles and narratives seamlessly. From walking puzzle adventures to spine-chilling mysteries, Osiris invites players into realms where every step is an enigma, and every moment, unforgettable.
